Dijital Qırım — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 September 2026
Version: 1.0

This document explains which personal data the Dijital Qırım app processes, why it processes them, and how you can exercise your rights. It is written to meet Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK) and the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

The Turkish text (gizlilik/tr.md) is the authoritative version.

In short

  • The app has no ads, no analytics, no trackers and no crash reporting.
  • We do not ask for location permission. The app never reads your phone's location.
  • Your data is stored on servers hosted in Germany (Frankfurt).
  • We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising.
  • You can use the app without an account, as a guest. The dictionary, cuisine, music, maps and bibliography all work without signing in.

1. Data controller

  • Data controller: Tolga Gürtuna
  • Contact: dijitalqirim@gmail.com
  • App: Dijital Qırım (com.dijitalqirim.app)

Please send data protection requests to the address above.

2. What we process

2.1 If you use the app without an account

None of your personal data is stored on our servers. Only the network requests needed for the app to work are made (see 4. Third parties).

2.2 If you create an account

  • E-mail address — from you, or from Google if you signed in with Google. To create your account and let you sign in.
  • Password — only when registering with e-mail. For authentication. The password is stored as a cryptographic hash; not even we can see it in clear text.
  • Full name, username — from you or from your Google profile. To identify you in the app.
  • Profile picture URL — from Google if you signed in with Google.
  • Country, city — from you, optional. To show your location in the diaspora network and in mentorship.
  • Preferred dialect — from you. For dictionary and content preferences.
  • Ancestral village — from you, optional. To link you to your village in the Tamır module.
  • Interface language — from you. To remember the app's language.
  • KVKK/GDPR consent and its date — from you. As a record of consent.

2.3 Data created as you use the app

  • Your learning progress (which words you knew or did not) — so the flashcard feature works.
  • Your family tree records — to store your family tree. See section 3.
  • Your mentorship entry and matches — so the mentorship network works. See section 5.
  • Your village council memberships — for participation in community pages.
  • The content reports you send and your block list — so that inappropriate content can be reviewed and so that people you have blocked are not shown to you. Your reports are seen only by you and by the people who run the app; the person reported is not told. Your block list is visible only to you and the blocked person is not notified.

3. Family tree records — data about other people

In the family tree module you enter not your own data but your relatives': names, birth and death years, birthplaces and notes. Those people may be alive.

We take this seriously:

  • Your family tree records are visible only to you. They are protected by a row-level security (RLS) rule in the database; another user cannot read them even if they hold the app's key.
  • These records are never published, never shown to other users, and never placed in a searchable index.
  • The responsibility is yours: when you enter data about a living relative, that person should know about it and consent to it. If a relative asks you to remove their record you can delete it in the app; write to us if you need help.

4. Third parties we share data with

We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising. The following services are involved in running the app:

Supabase (hosting, database, authentication, file storage)

All account and content data lives here. The servers are in the Amazon Web Services eu-central-1 (Frankfurt, Germany) region. → https://supabase.com/privacy

Google (only if you choose "Sign in with Google")

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google knows that you signed in and gives us your e-mail address, your name and your profile picture URL. If you register with e-mail and password, Google is not involved. → https://policies.google.com/privacy

OpenStreetMap (map tiles)

When you open the Tamır or Diaspora maps, the map images are downloaded from OpenStreetMap servers. In doing so your IP address and the map area you are looking at reach OpenStreetMap. This is a technically unavoidable consequence of displaying a map. → https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

External links

When you tap a link in a bibliography record or a music credit, your phone's browser opens and takes you to that site (archive.org, doi.org, publisher sites, kirimdernegi.org.tr, ru.wikipedia.org and similar). From that point on, that site's privacy policy applies.

5. Mentorship — what becomes visible

The mentorship network is optional and nothing about you is listed unless you register yourself.

  • If you register as a mentor: your name, username, city, country, dialect preference, area of expertise and the description you write become visible to other signed-in users. They are not visible to guests.
  • If you register as a mentee: you are not listed anywhere. Only the mentor you apply to can see you.
  • Your e-mail address is never shown to other users, under any circumstances.
  • You can delete your entry at any time; you disappear from the list immediately.

6. Where the data is stored, and international transfer

Your data is hosted in Germany (AWS Frankfurt).

  • Under KVKK: this constitutes a transfer of your personal data abroad. The explicit consent you give in order to use the app also covers this transfer.
  • Under GDPR: the data stays within the European Economic Area.

7. How long it is kept

  • Your account data is kept for as long as your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, family tree records, learning progress, mentorship entry and matches are deleted.
  • The app's cultural content (dictionary, recipes, settlement list, music credits, bibliography) is not personal data and is not deleted.

8. Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK and under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Learn whether your personal data is being processed, and request information about it
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used in line with that purpose
  • Know the third parties to whom the data is transferred, at home or abroad
  • Request rectification if the data is incomplete or incorrect
  • Request erasure
  • Obtain a copy of your data (portability)
  • Object to processing and withdraw the consent you gave

You can exercise these rights by writing to dijitalqirim@gmail.com. Requests are answered within 30 days at the latest.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority, or — in the European Union — with the data protection authority of the country you live in.

9. Security

  • All network traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored as hashes; the clear text is kept nowhere.
  • The database uses row-level security (RLS): your profile, family tree, learning progress and mentorship entry can only be read within your own session.
  • Cultural content tables are open to everyone read-only; content cannot be added or deleted with the app's key.
  • The mentor list and matches are open only to signed-in users and contain only the fields that need to be displayed; the profile table as a whole is never exposed to other people.

10. Permissions the app does not request

Dijital Qırım does not request: location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, your files, or phone state.

The permissions it does request, and why:

  • Internet, network state — to connect to the server and download maps and audio.
  • Notifications (Android 13+) — to show the media notification while music plays.
  • Background execution, keeping the screen awake — so music is not interrupted when the screen is off.
  • Bluetooth — so the play/pause buttons on headphones work.

11. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe data about your child has been entered, write to us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this document

If this document is updated, the effective date changes and significant changes are announced inside the app. Past versions of the text are kept in the project's code repository.
